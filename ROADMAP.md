# Roadmap

Principle: if it does not save time or improve decision making, it does not make the roadmap.

## v0.1 · FinanceOS (live)

Authentication, accounts, mapped CSV imports, rules, review, dashboard, import history/reversal, audit trail, two-user RLS tests.

## v0.2 · Finance workflows + Marketing backend (this branch, migrations 003/004 pending apply)

Implemented: classification and tax-review flags, project tagging, monthly and project reports, tax-flag CSV, clients/projects/invoices/subscriptions/assets screens, saved import templates; Marketing brand profiles, action plan, human-approved draft queue, audit trail, legacy backup import and JSON export.
Still open: password recovery, account editing, invoice PDF/email, matching invoice payments to bank transactions, audit browsing UI, account balances and reconciliation.

## v0.3

Marketing integrations (each needs your credentials and approval): server-side AI drafting that always lands as an unapproved draft, Google Search Console and Analytics read-only pulls that create evidence-backed actions. Receipt OCR, bank reconciliation, executive dashboard, cash flow forecasting, accountant-reviewed tax classifications.

## v1.0

Direct account connections where available, organizations and roles, CPA portal, mobile app and optional AI Chief of Staff.
